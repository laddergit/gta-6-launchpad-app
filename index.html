Before you buy

  • Mint authority is revoked. The 1,000,000,000 GTA6L supply is fixed — no new tokens can ever be minted.
  • Brand-new, thin market. This token launched recently and trades on the Pump.fun bonding curve, so price can move fast in both directions and large orders slip.
  • Memecoin risk. GTA6L has no guaranteed utility or returns. Only trade what you can afford to lose, and always verify the contract address above before buying anywhere else.
